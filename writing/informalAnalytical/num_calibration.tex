\subsection{Calibration}\label{\refsec:calibration}
The calibration is a nested fixed point. The inner loop solves the full politico-economic equilibrium path; the outer loop searches over the parameters that cannot be set without it, until the targets of \cref{\refmodelsec:calibration} hold:
\begin{subequations}\label{\refeq:calibration}
    \begin{align}
        \widehat{K/Y}_{t_0} &= n_{\text{yr}}\frac{s_{t_0-1}/\nu_{t_0}}{\left(s_{t_0-1}/\nu_{t_0}\right)^{\alpha} h_{t_0}^{1-\alpha}} = n_{\text{yr}}\frac{\alpha}{R_{t_0}} \label{\refeq:calibration:KY}\\
        \hat{\tau}_{t_0} &= \tau_{t_0} \label{\refeq:calibration:tau}\\
        \hat{\eta}_{0} &= \frac{z_0^{\eta}}{z_0^x}\frac{(1-\alpha)(1-\tau_{t_0})}{\Theta_{h,t_0}^{\alpha}\left(\frac{1-\alpha}{\Gamma_{h,t_0}^{\alpha}}\right)^{\frac{1}{1+\alpha\xi}}} \label{\refeq:calibration:eta0}\\
        \hat{X}_{0} &= \hat{\eta}_{0}\frac{\left(\frac{1-\alpha}{\Gamma_{h,t_0}^{\alpha}}\right)^{\frac{1}{1+\alpha\xi}}}{\left(\Theta_{h,t_0}z_0^x\right)^{1/\xi}} \label{\refeq:calibration:X0}
    \end{align}
\end{subequations}
where hats mark targets and $t_0$ is the baseline year. The discount factor is $\beta_{t,j} = \beta p_{t,j}$ with a common $\beta$. By the dominant terms of the Jacobian, $\beta$ carries the capital--output ratio, $\omega$ the tax rate and $\eta_0, X_0$ the informal income and hours, but the four equations are solved jointly.

\smalltitle{Why the capital--output ratio.}
The first target \eqref{\refeq:calibration:KY} carries the factor $n_{\text{yr}}=30$, the number of years in a model period: a period's output $Y_{t_0}$ is thirty years of production while $s_{t_0-1}/\nu_{t_0}$ is a stock, so the model's own ratio $K_{t_0}/Y_{t_0}$ is a thirtieth of the capital--output ratio that national accounts report. The savings rate
\begin{align}
    \text{sr}_{t_0} &= \frac{s_{t_0}}{\left(s_{t_0-1}/\nu_{t_0}\right)^{\alpha} h_{t_0}^{1-\alpha}} \label{\refeq:calibration:sr}
\end{align}
is reported but not targeted: with capital fully depreciating between periods, $s_{t_0}$ \emph{is} the next period's capital stock, so \eqref{\refeq:calibration:sr} is $K_{t_0+1}/Y_{t_0}$, a stock over a period's flow rather than the annual saving rate of the national accounts. The capital--output ratio is measured directly and depends on the predetermined stock $s_{t_0-1}$ alone.

\smalltitle{The procedure.}
Given a trial vector $\left(\beta, \omega, \eta_0, X_0\right)$, the inner loop refreshes the parameters derived from it, $\kappa$ and $\epsilon$, whose discounting involves $\beta$, and re-solves the politico-economic equilibrium path (\cref{\refsec:PEELOG} or \cref{\refsec:PEE}). The outer loop is a root finder on \eqref{\refeq:calibration}, each parameter mapped to the real line by a logarithm, so positivity holds without a bound; $\beta$ is not bounded above. The capital--output ratio, $\eta_0$ and $X_0$ enter the residual relative to their targets and the tax rate as a level gap, so the four residuals are of similar size. The inner solve cannot be shortened to the baseline period, since $\beta$ moves $s_{t_0-1}$ through every earlier period as well as $h_{t_0}$.
