## Ground rules

- **Order**: code (C1, C2), then one US run (R1, R3, R4 together), then C3 and R2 (Argentina under
  common X), then writing. C3 waits for **RKB's confirmation that the other session is finished with
  `python/InformalSavings/`**; if that has not come when step 4 is done, go to step 6 and leave step 5
  for later.
- **Conventions**: `CLAUDE.md`, `notes/paper_styleGuide.md` for any tex. Never hand-edit a `%% GENERATED`
  file; do not compile tex; **no commits and no pushes** (including `writing/overleaf.py`). RKB reviews
  the diff.
- **Machine**: `PYTHONUTF8=1`; python is `.venv\Scripts\python.exe`. Long runs are detached: a ps1 under
  `logs/` (gitignored, pattern in `logs/runArgPipeline0911.ps1`) launched with
  `Start-Process powershell -WindowStyle Hidden -File logs\<x>.ps1`, python inside it via
  `cmd /c "... > log 2>&1"`, one line per stage with its exit code appended to a pipeline log, `DONE` at
  the end. Watch the log with Monitor; never foreground-sleep.
- **One heavy job at a time** (8 cores). The other session's Argentina job counts: no heavy run starts
  before `logs/argPipeline0911.log` ends in `DONE`. Heavy: calibration sweeps, the CRRA ESC leg, the 2-D
  solves, `PermanentCRRA`. The fast tests and the smoke runs in steps 2–3 are not heavy.
- **While a run is in flight**, do not edit what it imports or executes (`python/US/*.py` except
  `test_*.py`, `python/paper/*.py`, and `python/InformalSavings/*.py` during R2).
- **The other session** owns `python/InformalSavings/`, the Argentina outputs and `logs/arg*` until RKB says
  otherwise. Never copy Argentina outputs into `writing/Paper`: which Argentina version the paper prints is
  RKB's decision once both exist (W2).
- `results/` is tracked in git: compare a rerun against the committed vintage with `git diff` /
  `git show HEAD:<path>`.

## STOP conditions (report and wait; do not work around)

1. The costless sequential FOC is not negative on all of θ ∈ [0,1] at some ρ ("three timings, one
   corner" in the introduction, conclusion and `sec:esc` is at stake).
2. The US run moves anything beyond what step 4 says may move.
3. The exact solver and the path iteration disagree by more than ±0.01 in θ or 2e-4 in τ.
4. `PermanentCRRA` fails beyond one clear-bug fix, or the permanent-timing reference numbers in TODO R3 do
   not reproduce (to ~1e-6).
5. A test that passes at HEAD fails.
6. Anything would require touching `data/*.xlsx`, a generated tex file, or (before RKB's go) the other
   session's files.
7. A run exceeds twice its budget with no progress in its log.
