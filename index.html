The online appendix

This online appendix accompanies Social Security Design and Its Political Support by Rasmus K. S. Berg and Martín Gonzalez-Eiras. It extends the paper’s three quantitative sections, on Argentina’s pension reform, on the rich economies with the pension design given and on the endogenous design. It shows the results across the intertemporal and the Frisch elasticity, the UK compared with the U.S., the alternative calibration of the taste for leisure, a second cost of redistribution, and the paths behind the paper’s readings in a single year. Every number is built by the paper’s own pipeline from the model’s results; none is typed.
